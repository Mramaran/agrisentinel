# Sample image sources

`healthy.jpg`, `early_blight.jpg`, `late_blight.jpg`, `yellow_leaf_curl.jpg` and
`ambiguous.jpg` are resized from the **PlantDoc dataset**
(<https://github.com/pratikkayal/PlantDoc-Dataset>, train split, tomato folders),
released under **CC BY 4.0**. Each file was resized to at most 800 px wide and
re-encoded as JPEG; no other modification was made.

> Singh, D., Jain, N., Jain, P., Kayal, P., Kumawat, S., Batra, N.
> *PlantDoc: A Dataset for Visual Plant Disease Detection.* CoDS-COMAD 2020.

PlantDoc is web-scraped and some of its images carry stock-agency watermarks.
The five files above were picked from unwatermarked originals so nothing in the
demo shows another company's mark.

| file | PlantDoc original |
|---|---|
| healthy.jpg | Tomato leaf / IMG_2061.JPG.jpg |
| early_blight.jpg | Tomato Early blight leaf / 126689-004-D622CD2F.jpg |
| late_blight.jpg | Tomato leaf late blight / tomato_D33a-Late-blight-015-OMAFHRT_zoom.jpg |
| yellow_leaf_curl.jpg | Tomato leaf yellow virus / 072.jpg |
| ambiguous.jpg | Tomato Early blight leaf / Diseased-Tomato-Plant.jpg |

`not_a_leaf.jpg` is generated procedurally (stony ground texture) by the demo
authors and carries no third-party rights.
